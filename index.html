1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games developed by Batuhan Öztürk ("we", "our", or "developer"), including Color Feast: Candy Sort Puzzle and our other mobile titles.

We respect your personal privacy. Our games are designed to provide fun and seamless entertainment without requiring user accounts or collecting personal contact information such as your real name, street address, or phone number.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a smooth gaming experience, balance difficulty, and maintain game stability, we collect non-personal, anonymized data:

  • Gameplay & Progression: Completed levels, moves, puzzle score records, earned virtual items, and booster inventories.
  • Anonymous Identifiers: Randomly generated User IDs (such as GameAnalytics User ID) and Device Identifiers (IDFA / IDFV) used for gameplay analytics, crash diagnostics, and advertising attribution.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Crash stack traces, memory usage, frame rate metrics, and device model/OS version.

3. In-App Purchases (IAP)

Our games may offer optional virtual items (e.g. extra lives, booster packages). All payments are handled securely by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Store. The developer never receives, processes, or stores your credit card, bank, or billing information.

4. Third-Party Services & Publisher Partnerships

We partner with industry-standard third-party analytics and advertising networks to provide and optimize our free-to-play puzzle games:

  • GameAnalytics: Difficulty balancing, retention rate analysis, and technical diagnostics. (Privacy Policy)
  • Advertising & Mediation Partners (AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads): Delivering rewarded video ads, interstitial ads, and measuring ad engagement. (Google Privacy, AppLovin Privacy)

5. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 and newer, we respect Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. We will only request access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) with your explicit consent. You can enable or disable tracking at any time via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our puzzle games are crafted for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe data has been collected inadvertently, please contact us for immediate data purging.

7. Data Retention & Security

We use industry-standard encryption for all network communications. Anonymized data is retained only as long as necessary for gameplay integrity, fraud prevention, and analytics optimization.

How to Delete Your Data

Our games do not require account registration, usernames, or passwords. Your gameplay data and virtual items are stored locally on your device or linked to anonymous installation identifiers.

Direct User Control

Because no personal account is created on an external server, you have direct, instantaneous control over deleting your data at any time without needing to wait for a support ticket.

Step 1: Delete Local Game Progress & Save Files

To completely delete your local progress, high scores, coins, and saved game state:

  • On iOS: Touch and hold the game app icon on your Home Screen > Tap Remove App > Tap Delete App.
  • On Android: Open device Settings > Apps > Select the game > Tap Storage & Cache > Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data (or uninstall the app).

Step 2: Reset Advertising & Tracking Identifiers

If you wish to reset or opt out of anonymous analytics and advertising identifiers:

  • On iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Toggle off permission for the game. (You can also go to Apple Advertising and toggle off Personalized Ads).
  • On Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
